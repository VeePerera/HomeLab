## Overview

A personal knowledge base of troubleshooting guides for common IT support issues. 

### Structure

Articles are organized by category:
- **Operating Systems** — Windows troubleshooting, updates, performance
- **Networking** — Wi-Fi, VPN, DNS, connectivity issues
- **Hardware** — Printers, laptops, monitors, peripherals
- **Software/Applications** — Outlook, Teams, Office issues
- **Account & Access** — Password resets, lockouts, permissions
